HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to Cardboard Flick Baseball, built directly from the real DIY cardboard prototype!

  • Swing the Bat: Click/drag the duct-tape wrapped bat backwards against the purple rubberband to build tension, then release to flick!
  • Pitch the Ball: Click PITCH BALL (or press Space / P) to roll a ball from the center mound slot toward home plate.
  • Aim for Pockets:
    • Pink HR: Corner pocket = HOME RUN! Clears the bases.
    • Yellow 3rd Base: Triple = Advance runners 3 bases.
    • Purple 2nd Base: Double = Advance runners 2 bases.
    • Orange 1st Base: Single = Advance runners 1 base.
    • Light Blue Out / Infield Hole: Adds an Out. 3 Outs ends the half-inning!
  • Batter in the Box: Toggle between Right-Handed (RHB) and Left-Handed (LHB) batter by clicking the 🧢 Batter button or pressing B! A centered swing through the plate drives the ball straight to Center Field!
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Space = Pitch, F / ↓ = Flick Bat, B = Switch Batter, N = Nudge, R = Reset ball.
